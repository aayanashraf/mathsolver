Cross Product Calculator

Find the cross product of two 3D vectors, its magnitude and a perpendicular check.

About this calculator

The cross product of two 3D vectors is a new vector perpendicular to both. Its length equals the area of the parallelogram they span.

How to use it

  1. Enter two vectors with three numbers each.
  2. Read each component calculation.
  3. Check that the result is perpendicular to both inputs.

Example

(1, 2, 3) × (4, 5, 6) = (−3, 6, −3).

Common questions

Does the order matter?

Yes. Swapping the vectors reverses the direction of the result.

When is the cross product zero?

When the vectors are parallel.

Does it work in 2D?

No, it is defined for 3D vectors.
