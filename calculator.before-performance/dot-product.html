Dot Product Calculator

Find the dot product of two vectors and the angle between them.

About this calculator

The dot product multiplies matching entries and adds them. A result of 0 means the vectors are perpendicular. This tool also finds the angle between them.

How to use it

  1. Enter both vectors with the same number of entries.
  2. Read the products and their sum.
  3. See the angle and what the sign means.

Example

(1, 2, 3) · (4, −5, 6) = 4 − 10 + 18 = 12.

Common questions

What does a dot product of 0 mean?

The vectors are perpendicular.

Do the vectors need the same length?

Yes. They must have the same number of entries.

Is the result a vector?

No, it is a single number.
