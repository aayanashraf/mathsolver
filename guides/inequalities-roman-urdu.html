Inequality Kaise Solve Karein (Roman Urdu Guide)

Inequality mein barabar ki jagah <, >, ≤ ya ≥ hota hai. Tareeqa linear equation jaisa hai, lekin ek khaas usool hai.

Sab se zaroori usool

Agar aap dono taraf ko negative number se zarb ya taqseem karein to nishan ulta ho jata hai (< se > aur > se <).

Misaal: -2x + 3 < 11

  1. Dono taraf se 3 ghatayein: -2x < 8
  2. Dono taraf ko -2 se taqseem karein aur nishan ulta karein: x > -4
  3. Check: x = 0 rakhein, to 3 < 11 sahi hai

Misaal: 3x - 5 ≥ 10

3x ≥ 15, to x ≥ 5. Yahan positive number se taqseem hui, is liye nishan wohi raha.

Khud try karein

-3x + 1 > 10 solve karein. Jawab: -3x > 9, to x < -3.
