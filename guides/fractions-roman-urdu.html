Fractions Ko Jama, Tafreeq, Zarb Aur Taqseem Karna (Roman Urdu)

Fraction mein upar wala hissa numerator aur neeche wala denominator kehlata hai.

Jama aur tafreeq

Pehle denominators ko barabar karein (LCM nikal kar), phir sirf numerators jama ya tafreeq karein.

Misaal: 3/4 + 5/6. 4 aur 6 ka LCM 12 hai. 3/4 = 9/12 aur 5/6 = 10/12. Jama: 9/12 + 10/12 = 19/12 = 1 7/12.

Misaal: 5/6 - 3/4 = 10/12 - 9/12 = 1/12.

Zarb

Numerator ko numerator se aur denominator ko denominator se zarb dein. 2/3 x 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5.

Taqseem

Dusre fraction ko ulta karein aur zarb dein. 2/3 divided by 4/5 = 2/3 x 5/4 = 10/12 = 5/6.

Khud try karein

1/2 + 2/3 solve karein. Jawab: 3/6 + 4/6 = 7/6 = 1 1/6.
