Percentage Kaise Nikalein (Roman Urdu Guide)

Percent ka matlab hai "har sau mein se". 15% ka matlab 100 mein se 15.

Formula

Hissa = (percent / 100) x poori value

Misaal 1: 240 ka 15% kya hoga?

  1. 15% ko decimal banayein: 15 / 100 = 0.15
  2. Zarb dein: 0.15 x 240 = 36

Misaal 2: 36, 240 ka kitna percent hai?

(36 / 240) x 100 = 0.15 x 100 = 15%

Misaal 3: 800 rupay par 20% discount

  1. Discount: 0.20 x 800 = 160
  2. Nayi qeemat: 800 - 160 = 640 rupay

Khud try karein

360 ka 25% nikalein. Jawab: 0.25 x 360 = 90.
