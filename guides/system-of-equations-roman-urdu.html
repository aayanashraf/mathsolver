Do Equations Ka System Kaise Solve Karein (Roman Urdu)

Jab do equations mein do unknown (x aur y) hon to unhein ek saath solve karte hain. Do aam tareeqe hain: elimination aur substitution.

Elimination: x + y = 10 aur x - y = 2

  1. Dono equations ko jama karein: 2x = 12
  2. x nikalein: x = 6
  3. Pehli equation mein rakhein: 6 + y = 10, to y = 4
  4. Check: 6 + 4 = 10 aur 6 - 4 = 2, dono sahi

Substitution (usi system par)

  1. x - y = 2 se x = y + 2
  2. Pehli equation mein rakhein: (y + 2) + y = 10, to 2y = 8, y = 4
  3. Phir x = 4 + 2 = 6

Khud try karein

x + y = 9 aur x - y = 1 solve karein. Jawab: x = 5, y = 4.
