Derivative Kaise Nikalein: Power Rule (Roman Urdu Guide)

Derivative batata hai ke koi function kitni tezi se badal raha hai. Sab se pehle power rule seekhein.

Power rule

Agar f(x) = xn ho to f'(x) = n xn-1. Yani power ko aage zarb dein aur power mein se 1 kam kar dein.

Misalein

  • x3 ka derivative 3x2
  • 5x4 ka derivative 5 x 4 x3 = 20x3
  • Constant (jaise 7) ka derivative 0 hota hai

Kai terms wala function

x2 + 3x + 7 ka derivative: har term ka alag nikalein. 2x + 3 + 0 = 2x + 3.

Khud try karein

2x3 - 4x ka derivative nikalein. Jawab: 6x2 - 4.
