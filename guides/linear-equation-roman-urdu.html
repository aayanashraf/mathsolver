Linear Equation Kaise Solve Karein (Solve for x, Roman Urdu)

Linear equation mein x ki power 1 hoti hai, jaise 3x + 7 = 22. Maqsad x ko akela karna hai.

Sab se zaroori usool

Barabari ke dono taraf wohi operation karein. Jo aap ek taraf se ghatayein ya dusri taraf bhi ghatayein, aur jis se ek taraf taqseem karein, dusri taraf bhi usi se taqseem karein.

Misaal: 3x + 7 = 22

  1. Dono taraf se 7 ghatayein: 3x = 15
  2. Dono taraf ko 3 se taqseem karein: x = 5
  3. Check: 3(5) + 7 = 15 + 7 = 22, sahi hai

Fraction wali misaal: x/3 + 4 = 9

  1. Dono taraf se 4 ghatayein: x/3 = 5
  2. Dono taraf ko 3 se zarb dein: x = 15

Khud try karein

2x + 9 = 25 solve karein. Jawab: 2x = 16, to x = 8.
