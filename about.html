MathSolveIQ ke baare mein

MathSolveIQ ek free math helper hai jo students ko sirf jawab nahi, balkay tareeqa samjhata hai. Aap sawal type kar sakte hain, bol sakte hain, ya photo / PDF upload kar sakte hain, aur har step ke saath rule aur wajah dekh sakte hain.

Kin ke liye

SAT, AP, O/A Level, FSc aur JEE style sawalon ke liye banaya gaya hai, arithmetic se calculus, linear algebra, statistics aur probability tak.

Hints aur Socratic mode

Agar aap khud sochna chahte hain to Hints only ya Socratic mode chunein. Is mein jawab nahi diya jata, sirf rehnumai milti hai.

Zubaan

Jawab English, Urdu, Hindi aur Roman Urdu mein mil sakta hai. Jis zubaan mein sawal likhein ge, usi mein jawab aayega.

Ehtiyat

AI se kabhi kabhi galti ho sakti hai, khaas tor par hisab mein. Har jawab ke aakhir mein Check line hoti hai, aur zaroori nateeje apni taraf se bhi verify kar lein.

Rabta
