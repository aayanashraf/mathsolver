About MathSolveIQ

A free math helper that teaches the method, not just the answer.

Why we built it

Many students copy an answer and still do not know how to solve the next problem. MathSolveIQ is built around a different idea: show each step, name the rule, and explain why it works, so you can do it yourself next time.

What you can do here

  • Ask the AI helper for a step-by-step solution, a few hints, or guiding questions only.
  • Plot graphs, solve quadratics and use a scientific calculator, unit converter and statistics tools, all in your browser.
  • Practise with quizzes and exam-style sets for SAT, FSc and JEE.
  • Look up formulas quickly.

Made with students in mind

We care about students who study in English, Urdu, Hindi and Roman Urdu, and about exams like FSc, O and A Level, SAT and JEE. We are adding more languages and topics step by step.

Our promises

  • Honest about AI. It can make mistakes, so we remind you to check important work.
  • No account needed for the core tools.
  • Your privacy. Read how we handle data in our Privacy Policy.
