Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

MathSolveIQ is a free math helper for students. This page explains what happens to the information you give us. We keep it short and honest.

What you type or upload

When you press Solve, the problem you typed is sent to a third-party AI service so it can write the solution. If you upload a photo or a PDF, that file is also sent to an AI service, only to read the problem and answer it. Because of this, please do not put personal details, passwords or private documents in a problem.

What stays in your browser

  • Your recent problems and history are saved in your own browser (local storage) so you can reopen them.
  • Your theme choice (light or dark) is saved the same way.
  • Clearing your browser data removes all of it. We cannot see it.

The graph, quadratic, calculator, unit converter and statistics tools run in your browser and do not send anything to an AI service.

Accounts

You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email or date of birth to use the tools.

Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most hosts, Vercel and our AI provider may process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and request time to deliver the service, prevent abuse and keep it reliable.

Analytics and cookies

We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell personal data. If we add analytics in the future, we will say so on this page first.

Children

MathSolveIQ is made for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and teachers who think a child has shared personal details in a problem can contact us and we will help.

AI can be wrong

Answers are generated by AI and may contain mistakes. Check important work yourself, especially for exams and homework.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
