Slope Calculator

Find the slope and line equation from two points.

About this calculator

Slope is rise over run: how much a line goes up for every step across. Enter two points and get the slope, the intercept and the equation.

How to use it

  1. Enter the coordinates of both points.
  2. Read the slope formula with your numbers.
  3. Get the equation y = mx + b.

Example

Points (1, 2) and (4, 8): slope = 6/3 = 2, so y = 2x + 0.

Common questions

What if the line is vertical?

The change in x is 0, so the slope is undefined and the line is x = a number.

What does a negative slope mean?

The line goes down as you move to the right.

What is the y-intercept?

The value of y where the line crosses the y-axis.
