Mean, Median and Mode Calculator

Find the mean, median, mode and range of a data set.

About this calculator

Mean, median and mode are three ways to describe the centre of data. This tool sorts your numbers and shows how each one is found, plus the range.

How to use it

  1. Enter numbers separated by commas.
  2. Check the sorted list.
  3. Read the mean, median, mode and range.

Example

3, 7, 7, 2, 9, 10, 7: sorted 2, 3, 7, 7, 7, 9, 10. Median 7, mode 7, mean about 6.43.

Common questions

What is the difference between mean and median?

The mean is the average of all values. The median is the middle value after sorting, so big outliers affect it less.

Can there be no mode?

Yes. If every value appears once, there is no mode.

Can there be more than one mode?

Yes. If two values tie for most frequent, both are modes.
