Quadratic Equation Calculator

Solve ax² + bx + c = 0 with the discriminant and a check.

About this calculator

A quadratic equation can have two real roots, one repeated root or two complex roots. This calculator uses the quadratic formula and shows the discriminant that decides which case you have.

How to use it

  1. Enter a, b and c.
  2. Read the discriminant.
  3. See the roots, the check and the vertex.

Example

x² − 5x + 6 = 0: D = 1, roots x = 2 and x = 3.

Common questions

What does the discriminant tell me?

If it is positive there are two real roots, if zero one repeated root, if negative two complex roots.

When can I factor instead?

When two numbers multiply to ac and add to b. Factoring is faster when it works.

What is the vertex?

The turning point of the parabola.
