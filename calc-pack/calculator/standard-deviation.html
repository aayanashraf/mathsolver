Standard Deviation Calculator

Calculate sample or population standard deviation with a full deviation table.

About this calculator

Standard deviation tells you how spread out data is around its mean. This calculator shows the mean, each deviation, the variance and the final square root.

How to use it

  1. Enter your data separated by commas.
  2. Choose Sample or Population.
  3. Read the table and the four steps.

Example

Data 4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42: mean 18, sample variance 164.4, sample standard deviation about 12.82.

Common questions

Sample or population?

Use population when you have every value in the group. Use sample when your data is only a part of a bigger group.

Why divide by n − 1 for a sample?

A sample tends to underestimate the spread, and dividing by n − 1 corrects that.

What does a large standard deviation mean?

The values are spread far from the mean.
