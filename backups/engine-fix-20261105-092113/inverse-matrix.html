Inverse Matrix Calculator

Find the inverse of a 2x2 or 3x3 matrix and check it by multiplication.

About this calculator

The inverse of a matrix A is the matrix that gives the identity when multiplied by A. It exists only when the determinant is not 0. This tool shows the determinant, the adjugate and a final check.

How to use it

  1. Enter a square matrix, like 2,1;5,3.
  2. Read the determinant and the cofactor or adjugate steps.
  3. Confirm the check: A times its inverse gives the identity.

Example

For 2,1;5,3 the determinant is 1, the adjugate is 3,−1;−5,2, so the inverse is 3,−1;−5,2.

Common questions

When does an inverse not exist?

When the determinant is 0. The tool tells you the matrix is singular.

How is a 3x3 inverse found?

With cofactors: build the cofactor matrix, transpose it to get the adjugate, then divide by the determinant.

How do I check the answer?

Multiply A by the inverse. You should get the identity matrix.
