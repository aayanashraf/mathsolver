Inequality Calculator

Solve ax + b < c (and the other signs) with sign flips and interval notation.

About this calculator

Solving a linear inequality works like solving an equation, with one extra rule: dividing or multiplying by a negative number flips the sign. This tool shows the flip and writes the answer in interval notation.

How to use it

  1. Enter a, b and c and pick the sign.
  2. Follow the subtract and divide steps.
  3. Read the answer and its interval.

Example

−2x + 3 < 11 gives x > −4, written (−4, ∞).

Common questions

Why does the sign flip?

Multiplying by a negative reverses the order of numbers on the number line.

What do round and square brackets mean?

Round brackets leave the endpoint out. Square brackets include it.

What if x cancels?

The statement is either always true or never true.
