Derivative Calculator

Differentiate sums of power, sin, cos, e^x and ln terms with the rule for each term.

About this calculator

A derivative measures how fast a function changes. This calculator handles sums of simple terms and names the rule used on each one. For products, quotients and chains, ask the AI helper on the home page.

How to use it

  1. Type f(x) as terms joined by + or −, such as 3x^4 − 2x^2 + 5x − 7.
  2. Read the rule applied to each term.
  3. Combine the results into f′(x).

Example

f(x) = 3x⁴ − 2x² + 5x − 7 gives f′(x) = 12x³ − 4x + 5.

Common questions

What is the power rule?

The derivative of xⁿ is n times x to the power n − 1.

What can this calculator not do?

Products, quotients and nested functions such as sin(2x).

What is the derivative of a constant?

Zero.
