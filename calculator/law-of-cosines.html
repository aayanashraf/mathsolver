Law of Cosines Calculator

Find a missing side or angle of a triangle with the law of cosines.

About this calculator

The law of cosines extends the Pythagorean theorem to any triangle. Use it when you know two sides and the angle between them, or all three sides.

How to use it

  1. Choose what you know.
  2. Enter the sides and angle.
  3. Read the substitution and the result.

Example

Sides 5 and 7 with a 60° angle between them give c ≈ 6.245.

Common questions

When do I use it instead of the sine rule?

When you know two sides and the included angle, or all three sides.

What happens at 90°?

cos 90° = 0 and it becomes the Pythagorean theorem.

Can three sides make any triangle?

No. Each side must be shorter than the other two added together.
