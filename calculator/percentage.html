Percentage Calculator

Find a percent of a number, what percent one number is of another, and percent change.

About this calculator

Percent questions all use the same idea: a percent is a fraction out of 100. This calculator handles three common cases and writes out the working.

How to use it

  1. Pick the kind of question.
  2. Enter X and Y.
  3. Follow the steps to see how the answer is built.

Example

20% of 150: 20/100 × 150 = 30.

Common questions

How do I find a percent of a number?

Divide the percent by 100, then multiply by the number.

How is percent change calculated?

Subtract the old value from the new one, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100.

Why can the old value not be 0?

Percent change divides by the starting value, and you cannot divide by 0.
