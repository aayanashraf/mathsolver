Distance Formula Calculator

Find the distance and midpoint between two points.

About this calculator

The distance formula is the Pythagorean theorem in coordinate form. This tool shows the substitution, the exact radical and the decimal, and also gives the midpoint.

How to use it

  1. Enter two points.
  2. See the differences squared and added.
  3. Read the exact and decimal distance.

Example

Points (0, 0) and (3, 4): distance = √25 = 5.

Common questions

Why does it use squares and a square root?

It comes from the Pythagorean theorem applied to the horizontal and vertical gaps.

Does the order of the points matter?

No. Squaring removes the sign, so the distance is the same.

What is the midpoint formula?

Average the x-values and average the y-values.
