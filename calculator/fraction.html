Fraction Calculator

Add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions with every step shown.

About this calculator

Fractions can feel slow because you must find common denominators and simplify at the end. This calculator shows each of those moves so you can follow the method and repeat it on paper.

How to use it

  1. Type the numerator and denominator of both fractions.
  2. Choose +, -, × or ÷.
  3. Read the steps: common denominator, the operation, then simplifying.

Example

1/2 + 1/3: the common denominator is 6, so you add 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

Common questions

Why do I need a common denominator to add fractions?

Because you can only add parts of the same size. Rewriting both fractions with the same denominator makes the pieces the same size.

How do I divide fractions?

Flip the second fraction and multiply. Dividing by 2/3 is the same as multiplying by 3/2.

Does it simplify the answer?

Yes. It divides top and bottom by their greatest common factor and also shows a mixed number and a decimal.
