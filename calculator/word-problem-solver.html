Word Problem Solver

Guided solver for 12 kinds of word problems: speed and distance, percentages, interest, ages, work rates, number problems and more, with the equation and every step.

About this calculator

Most word problems follow a small number of patterns. Choose the pattern, put the numbers from your problem into the boxes, and the solver writes the equation, solves it step by step and checks the answer. Each type is explained in the guide below.

How to use it

  1. Choose the type of problem that matches yours.
  2. Fill in Value 1, 2, 3 (and 4 if the type uses it) with the numbers from your problem, using the guide below. Leave a box blank only when the type says it is optional or is the one to find.
  3. Read the equation that is set up, each solving step and the check at the end.

Example

A train travels 120 km in 2 hours. Choose Speed, distance and time, leave Value 1 blank, enter 120 and 2. The speed is 120 / 2 = 60 km per hour.

Common questions

Which problems can it solve?

Speed, distance and time; two travellers meeting or one catching up; percentages and finding an original amount; simple and compound interest; two numbers from their sum and difference; consecutive numbers; ages; working together; two kinds of items; and rectangle sides.

My problem is worded differently. What should I do?

Pick the closest type and match your numbers to the boxes with the guide. For a problem written in free-form sentences, ask the AI helper on the home page.

Does it show the working?

Yes. Every answer shows how the equation is set up, the solving steps and a check.
