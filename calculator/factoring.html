Factoring Calculator

Factor ax² + bx + c into integer factors, with the common factor and roots shown.

About this calculator

Factoring rewrites a quadratic as a product. This calculator takes out the common factor, tests the discriminant and then builds the factors from the roots.

How to use it

  1. Enter a, b and c as whole numbers.
  2. See the common factor and discriminant.
  3. Read the factored form and the check.

Example

x² − 5x + 6 factors as (x − 2)(x − 3).

Common questions

What if it does not factor?

If the discriminant is not a perfect square, the roots are not rational. Use the quadratic formula.

Why take out the GCF first?

It makes the numbers smaller and the factoring easier.

How do I check a factoring answer?

Expand the factors and compare with the original.
