GCF and LCM Calculator

Find the greatest common factor and least common multiple using prime factorisation.

About this calculator

The GCF is the biggest number that divides all your numbers. The LCM is the smallest number they all divide into. Both come straight from prime factorisation, which this tool lays out for you.

How to use it

  1. Enter two or more whole numbers separated by commas.
  2. See each number written as a product of primes.
  3. Read how the GCF and LCM are taken from those primes.

Example

12 and 18: 12 = 2² × 3 and 18 = 2 × 3². GCF = 2 × 3 = 6 and LCM = 2² × 3² = 36.

Common questions

What is the difference between GCF and LCM?

GCF is the largest shared factor. LCM is the smallest shared multiple.

When do I use the LCM?

When adding fractions, to find a common denominator, or when two events repeat on different cycles.

Can I enter more than two numbers?

Yes, up to eight whole numbers.
