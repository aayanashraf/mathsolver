Privacy Policy

MathSolveIQ does not store your private uploaded photos, PDF math sheets, or personal inputs on external servers. All math processing runs securely in client-side runtime.

AI services and uploads

Problems you type are sent to a third-party AI service to generate the solution. Photos and PDFs you upload are sent to a third-party AI service (Google) only to read the problem text. Your recent problems are stored in your own browser, and we do not ask you to create an account.
